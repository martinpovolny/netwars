golden vector generator

Runs shared/sim/world.js#stepWorld for a fixed number of ticks off a seeded RNG and serialises the world after each tick. Save the result as server/testdata/golden.json; the Go parity test replays the same seed and asserts its stepWorld matches within 1e-4.

Regenerate only after a deliberate change to shared/sim/. The Go test will then fail until server/game is re-synced — that is the drift guard doing its job.
